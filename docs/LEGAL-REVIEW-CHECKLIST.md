## D. Citation table, checked against the published code (CLOSE-02, 2026-10-06)

Written to make the counsel review a walk-through rather than a hunt. **Not legal advice.** Where a row here differs from section A, this section is the one checked against the statute text as published; A was written from memory and its flags are superseded by the rows below.

**Source and method.** Every quotation is copied from the Texas Legislature's own statute files (`tcss.legis.texas.gov/resources/<CODE>/htm/<CODE>.<ch>.htm`, the files the statutes site renders), fetched 2026-10-06 and current through the 89th Legislature's regular session (2025). Case law and secondary sources are named as such. Confidence is per row:

- **High** — the quoted text answers the question directly.
- **Medium** — the statute is on point but the seeded value's mapping to it needs a judgement counsel has to make, or the finding rests on a case or a secondary source.
- **Low** — the row rests on *absence*: a text search of the chapter found no provision. Absence in one chapter is not absence in the law.
- **Blank** — nothing found worth citing. A blank beats a confident wrong citation.
